What a session costs

Two formats, and every session type fits into one of them. These are starting points — tell me what you have in mind and I'll quote it properly.

Mini session

from $75

$20 non-refundable deposit to book

A short, focused session. Good for announcements, cake smashes, holiday minis, and quick family updates.

  • Short and focused, one location
  • Edited images in a private online gallery
  • Print release included

Full session

from $150

20% non-refundable deposit to book

The unhurried version. Room to move between locations, change outfits, and let small children be small children.

Good to know

Every session is different

Length, how many images you get back, and how quickly they land all depend on what we're doing — a cake smash and a four-generation family shoot aren't the same job. I'll confirm all of it in writing when we book, so there are no surprises either way.

Booking

A non-refundable deposit holds your date — $20 for a mini, 20% for a full session — and comes off the final balance. Newborn dates are held loosely around your due date and firmed up once baby arrives.

Need to move your date? Tell me as early as you can and I'll do my best to carry your deposit across to the new one.

Travel

I'm a traveling photographer — Colorado and beyond. Sessions around Colorado Springs have no travel fee. Further out, I'll quote travel up front, before you commit to anything.
