About

Hi, I'm Nicole

I'm a Colorado-based traveling photographer, wife and mama, and I'm here for the genuine, beautiful moments that make your story uniquely yours.

Families and couples, engagements, maternity and newborns — I love all of it. What I'm really after in any session is the timeless image, the one you'll still want on the wall in twenty years.

My goal is to make every session feel relaxed, fun, and true to you. Nobody is on a clock, nobody has to perform. We turn life's little moments into something you can hold onto.

I'm based in Colorado Springs and I travel — Colorado and beyond. My work has been featured in Shoutout Colorado.

What a session is like

Before

We talk through what you want, where, and what everyone's wearing. I'll send a short guide so nobody is guessing the night before.

During

Mostly I follow you around. A handful of posed frames for the people who want one on the wall, and the rest is whatever actually happens.

After

Edited photos land in a private online gallery. Download them, print them, send them to grandparents.
